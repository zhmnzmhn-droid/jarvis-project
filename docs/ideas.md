# Zombie Survival Stick-Figure Episode Ideas

30 short-form episode ideas for a 2D stick-figure zombie survival channel (YouTube Shorts and TikTok).

Format per idea:

- Setting
- Danger
- Choice
- Turn
- Hook

---

### 1. Quiet street, loud problem

* Setting: street
* Danger: A single footstep echoes across an empty road and every nearby head turns toward it.
* Choice: The survivor freezes and mimics slow, calm breathing instead of running.
* Turn: The horde parts around them like water around a rock.
* Hook: "If you run, you die. If you freeze, maybe you live."

### 2. The van that won't start

* Setting: van
* Danger: The starter clicks but the engine stays dead outside a growing crowd of shapes.
* Choice: The survivor leaves the van and sprints through the alley instead.
* Turn: The horde follows the van, not the runner, and the alley clears in seconds.
* Hook: "My van died and the dead won't stop moving."

### 3. Window light mistake

* Setting: house
* Danger: A lamp left on upstairs glows through the curtain and the crowd below is staring up.
* Choice: The survivor knocks it out with a pillow while the crowd gathers.
* Turn: One shape breaks off the group and starts climbing up the porch.
* Hook: "I made one small mistake that cost us everything."

### 4. Rooftop rope rescue

* Setting: rooftop
* Danger: A friend is trapped on the lower ledge and the rope is fraying fast.
* Choice: The survivor lowers the rope one meter at a time instead of letting it fall free.
* Turn: The friend pulls themselves up but the frayed section holds only by a thread.
* Hook: "Hold the rope or lose your friend."

### 5. The quiet forest path

* Setting: forest
* Danger: Branches snap ahead in the trees and the sounds are coming closer.
* Choice: The survivor steps off the path and crawls through the underbrush.
* Turn: A low growl follows them from every direction at once.
* Hook: "In the woods, every footstep can end you."

### 6. Rooftop water crisis

* Setting: rooftop
* Danger: The water jug is empty and the sunrise is minutes away.
* Choice: The survivor offers their last ration to a rival survivor instead of keeping it.
* Turn: The rival trades them a working radio and a map to the nearest clean source.
* Hook: "I had nothing to trade but I risked everything."

### 7. Stairwell trap

* Setting: house
* Danger: The hallway door is jammed and shapes are pounding through the floorboards below.
* Choice: The survivor climbs the stairwell and blocks the top landing with a shelf.
* Turn: The shelf holds for hours, but the floor below starts to crack.
* Hook: "We had one floor between us and them."

### 8. Noise in the night

* Setting: street
* Danger: A loud crash from a dumpster wakes every shape on the block.
* Choice: The survivor throws a can far away to pull the crowd toward the sound.
* Turn: They are alone for only seconds before the crowd realizes the trick.
* Hook: "A noise at night is never just a noise."

### 9. Van window cracked

* Setting: van
* Danger: A hairline crack in the windshield is growing with every mile they drive.
* Choice: The survivor parks under a bridge instead of pushing on with the broken window.
* Turn: A lone shape is sheltering under the same bridge, unaware of the approaching van.
* Hook: "A tiny crack can tear your whole plan apart."

### 10. Rooftop fire alarm

* Setting: rooftop
* Danger: A false alarm is sounding and the shapes below are climbing toward the source.
* Choice: The survivor climbs higher to reach the control panel and kill the alarm.
* Turn: The panel is dead, so the alarm will keep ringing until someone else fixes it.
* Hook: "The alarm saved me once and now it's hunting me."

### 11. The creaky floorboard

* Setting: house
* Danger: One floorboard in the hallway groans under every weight placed on it.
* Choice: The survivor crawls across instead of walking, even though it is slower.
* Turn: A shape is standing just beyond the end of the board, waiting.
* Hook: "One floorboard stood between us and the end of the road."

### 12. Forest camp rescue

* Setting: forest
* Danger: A stranger's campfire is still warm and their gear is scattered.
* Choice: The survivor follows the scattered gear instead of the trail.
* Turn: The "stranger" is tied up under a tree, alive, and waiting for someone to come back.
* Hook: "I found a camp that was just warm enough to worry me."

### 13. Streetlight flicker

* Setting: street
* Danger: A streetlight flickers and the crowd of shapes freezes every time it goes out.
* Choice: The survivor times their dash to the moments when the light is solid.
* Turn: The light finally dies for good, and the shapes begin to move freely.
* Hook: "The light was my only friend in the dark."

### 14. Van engine noise

* Setting: van
* Danger: The engine idles with a low rumble and the crowd is drifting toward it.
* Choice: The survivor kills the engine and backs the van by hand.
* Turn: A shape stands in the alley exit, blocking the only clear path.
* Hook: "My own engine was drawing them to me."

### 15. Rooftop rope ladder

* Setting: rooftop
* Danger: The rope ladder is the only way down and it is already tangled.
* Choice: The survivor cuts the tangles section by section, working with one hand on the ledge.
* Turn: The cut section drops through the gap below and lands softly on the pavement.
* Hook: "One tangled rope could end my whole run."

### 16. The house that moved

* Setting: house
* Danger: The front door is open and a shape is standing just inside, unmoving.
* Choice: The survivor slips in through the window behind the shape instead of going around.
* Turn: The shape turns with startling speed, as if it was waiting for them.
* Hook: "I opened the wrong door in the wrong house."

### 17. Forest bridge

* Setting: forest
* Danger: A wooden bridge is the only crossing and its planks are loose.
* Choice: The survivor carries their friend across one plank at a time.
* Turn: Halfway over, a loose plank falls and the friend's grip gives out.
* Hook: "One bad plank on the bridge could break us both."

### 18. Rooftop pigeon problem

* Setting: rooftop
* Danger: A pigeon takes off with a loud clatter and the crowd turns toward the noise.
* Choice: The survivor throws a stone at the pigeon instead of hiding.
* Turn: The pigeon misses, and the crowd's attention is now fully locked on them.
* Hook: "A pigeon ruined my quiet plan in one second."

### 19. The quiet street test

* Setting: street
* Danger: A test of how long the street stays silent decides whether they move on.
* Choice: The survivor stays still on the corner for a full minute without breathing hard.
* Turn: A shape stops a few feet away and tilts its head like it is listening.
* Hook: "I had to stay quiet to live another day."

### 20. Van spare tire

* Setting: van
* Danger: The spare tire is flat and the road is filling with shapes.
* Choice: The survivor uses the tire as a shield while pushing the van forward.
* Turn: The tire skids and rolls into the crowd, opening a clear lane.
* Hook: "My last tire was the only shield I had left."

### 21. House blackout

* Setting: house
* Danger: The power is out and the window is the brightest spot in the dark room.
* Choice: The survivor covers the window with a blanket and waits.
* Turn: The blanket shifts, and a shape presses its face against the glass.
* Hook: "In the dark, a blanket is the thinnest wall you have."

### 22. Rooftop fire escape

* Setting: rooftop
* Danger: The fire escape is the only way down and the lower rungs are bent.
* Choice: The survivor descends two rungs at a time, skipping the bent ones.
* Turn: At the ground floor, the door on the other side is locked from the outside.
* Hook: "The escape was only half the problem."

### 23. Forest clearing rescue

* Setting: forest
* Danger: A trapped friend is in a clearing and the shapes are closing in from the trees.
* Choice: The survivor throws a can into the trees to pull the crowd away.
* Turn: The friend follows the can instead of the survivor's signal.
* Hook: "I threw one can to save my friend's life."

### 24. The street that echoed

* Setting: street
* Danger: Every footstep on the old pavement echoes and the crowd can hear it.
* Choice: The survivor walks only on the soft patches where the pavement is broken.
* Turn: The broken patch gives way under their weight.
* Hook: "The street was listening to every step I took."

### 25. Van mirror trick

* Setting: van
* Danger: The mirror shows a shape right behind the van, but it is not moving.
* Choice: The survivor reverses slowly and watches the shape instead of stopping.
* Turn: The shape opens its mouth and screams at the van.
* Hook: "The mirror told me I was never alone."

### 26. House attic plan

* Setting: house
* Danger: The attic is above the crowd's reach but the door is stuck.
* Choice: The survivor wedges the door open from inside while the crowd pounds below.
* Turn: The door finally gives, but the shape just below is reaching up.
* Hook: "One stuck door kept us from the top floor."

### 27. Rooftop radio signal

* Setting: rooftop
* Danger: A radio call is broadcasting their location to everyone nearby.
* Choice: The survivor throws the radio off the roof instead of continuing to listen.
* Turn: A second radio signal starts from the building across the street.
* Hook: "The radio saved me until it stopped."

### 28. The forest noise plan

* Setting: forest
* Danger: A noise-making plan to distract the crowd is failing because the sound is too soft.
* Choice: The survivor uses a branch to make a loud, clear snap on purpose.
* Turn: The crowd splits and one group heads straight for the survivor's hiding spot.
* Hook: "My own noise plan was pulling me into the trap."

### 29. Street crossing point

* Setting: street
* Danger: The safest crossing point is blocked by a shape standing still.
* Choice: The survivor waits, and crosses only when the shape steps off to the side.
* Turn: The shape's step is the warning, not the danger, and the crowd is already behind them.
* Hook: "I crossed when I should have been hiding."

### 30. Van roof hideout

* Setting: van
* Danger: The van's roof is the only safe spot and a shape is climbing the side.
* Choice: The survivor drops from the roof onto the pavement while the shape reaches the top.
* Turn: The shape stays on the roof and the survivor is now on the ground, alone.
* Hook: "My last safe spot was the roof of a van."
